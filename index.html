Technical Deep Dive

NEV Assembly Process

From individual cells to complete vehicles — every step demands precision

🔋
Battery Pack Assembly
Cell-to-module-to-pack integration with BMS, thermal management, and sealing
▼
1
Cell Sorting & Validation
Incoming cells tested for voltage, impedance, and capacity matching within ±2mV tolerance
2
Module Assembly
Cells connected in series/parallel via laser or ultrasonic welding to form modules
3
BMS Integration
Battery Management System sensors installed for real-time monitoring of voltage, temperature, SoC
4
Thermal Management
Thermal interface materials applied; cooling plates integrated for temperature regulation
5
Pack Sealing & EOL Test
Gaskets applied, cover fastened; insulation resistance, high-voltage interlock, and leak tests
⚙️
Electric Motor & Controller
Three-in-one powertrain: motor + reducer + inverter with micron-level precision
▼
1
Stator Winding
Automated coil winding and insertion into stator lamination stack using robotic arms
2
Rotor Assembly
Permanent magnets press-fitted into rotor; dynamic balancing to <0.5g residual imbalance
3
Inverter/MCU Build
Power semiconductors (SiC/IGBT) soldered; control board assembled in sealed housing
4
3-in-1 Integration
Motor, reducer, and inverter integrated as single unit; HV bus connections made
5
Dyno Testing
Full-load dynamometer test: torque, efficiency, thermal performance validated
💧
Hydrogen Fuel Cell Stack
MEA stacking with precision alignment and helium leak verification
▼
1
MEA Preparation
Membrane Electrode Assembly: catalyst-coated membrane sandwiched with GDLs
2
Bipolar Plate Machining
Flow channel patterns machined or stamped into graphite/metal plates
3
Stack Assembly
200-400 cells stacked with precise alignment; compressed between end plates
4
Balance of Plant
Air compressor, hydrogen manifolds, humidifier, and coolant pump integrated
5
Leak & Performance Test
Helium leak test at 3 bar; electrochemical conditioning; power output validation
🚗
Vehicle Integration
Final powertrain marriage with HV connections and software calibration
▼
1
Chassis Prep
Skateboard platform prepared with mounting points and harness routing
2
Powertrain Marriage
Battery and motor installed via AGVs from below the chassis
3
HV Wiring & Busbars
High-voltage (up to 800V) connections with torque-controlled fastening
4
Software Flash & Calibration
VCU, BMS, MCU firmware flashed; drive parameters calibrated on rolling road
5
Final QC & Road Test
ADAS calibration, water ingress test, 20km road test, delivery inspection
